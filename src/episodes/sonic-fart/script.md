**Title card:** WHAT IF YOU / FARTED / AT THE SPEED OF SOUND?
What would happen if you farted at 767 miles per hour? That's the speed of sound.

**01 / SOUND BARRIER:** First, an incredibly loud blast. If the gas exceeded the speed of sound, it could even generate a tiny sonic boom. Congratulations, your backside has just broken the sound barrier.

**02 / UNDER PRESSURE:** But things get worse. To accelerate gas to that speed, your body would need to generate enormous pressure. You could suffer catastrophic internal injuries before the fart even escaped.

**03 / INDESTRUCTIBLE:** But let's assume your body was somehow indestructible. Would you launch yourself across the room? Unfortunately, no.

**04 / WORST ROCKET EVER:** Even at the speed of sound, a normal fart contains far too little mass to propel you any meaningful distance. You'd become the world's most disappointing rocket engine.

**05 / THE FALLOUT:** You wouldn't break the laws of physics, you'd probably just break your trousers, and you'd never be invited to a family dinner again.

Physics on screen: a fart is about 100 mL of gas, ≈ 0.1 g. At 343 m/s that is 0.034 N·s of momentum, which
pushes a 70 kg person at ≈ 0.5 mm/s (before friction), slower than a garden snail. Sonic (choked) flow
through an opening needs roughly twice the outside pressure behind it, far more than a gut can hold.
