**Title card:** WHAT IF YOU COULD / SELL YEARS / OF YOUR LIFE?
You have £10 left in your bank account. But there's a new way to make money. Sell five years of your
life for £500,000. Would you do it?

**01 / TIME IS MONEY:** If humans could sell their lifespan, time would become the most valuable currency on Earth.

**02 / THE FIRST SALES:** At first, millions would sell a few years to buy houses, pay off debts and escape poverty.

**03 / BUYING CENTURIES:** But billionaires wouldn't buy cars and private islands. They'd buy centuries of extra life.

**04 / TWO CLASSES:** Humanity would split into two classes. The rich, who could live for thousands of years. And the poor, who might never reach 40.

**05 / THE YEAR MARKET:** People would sacrifice decades just to feed their families. Parents would sell their years to give their children a better future.

**06 / HOW LONG YOU LIVE:** Money wouldn't just determine how well you live. It would determine how long you live. The rich wouldn't just own the world. They'd outlive everyone in it.

**07 / YOUR OFFER:** So, if someone offered you £1 million for ten years of your life… would you accept?
