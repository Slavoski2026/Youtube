**Title card:** WHAT IF EVERY / FISH / LEARNED TO WALK?
At exactly 8 AM, every fish on Earth suddenly learns how to walk.

**01 / THE FIRST STEP:** At first, nobody notices. Until a salmon climbs out of the river… and casually walks into a supermarket.

**02 / LANDFALL:** Within three hours, millions of fish begin crawling out of oceans, rivers, and lakes. Beaches are completely overrun.

**03 / THE SHARKS:** Then the sharks start coming. Great whites drag themselves through coastal towns.

**04 / EMPTY SEAS:** By day two, the fishing industry has collapsed. Boats return to empty waters.

**05 / THE MARCH:** But the fish aren't wandering randomly. They're heading somewhere. By day three, enormous lines of fish are marching towards the world's major cities.

**06 / UNDER SIEGE:** Swordfish smash through shop windows. Sharks gather outside government buildings. Humanity prepares for the worst.

**07 / TEN SECONDS:** But then, every fish on Earth suddenly stops moving. For ten seconds, complete silence.

**08 / PAYBACK:** Then billions of fish turn towards the nearest human… and begin walking faster. Because apparently, they've finally figured out who's been eating them for the last 10,000 years.
