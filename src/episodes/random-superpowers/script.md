# What If Every Human Got a Random Superpower? (ep. 8)

60 s cut of the user's script (approved). Take: Mark, 1 take, 58.3 s. Spend: voiceover 899 + Scribe 319 = 1,218 credits.

**Cold open / title**
Eight billion humans have just received a random superpower. Your neighbour can fly. Your best friend can teleport. But you can only change the colour of your socks.

**01 / PURE CHAOS**
Within hours: chaos. Super strength could accidentally destroy buildings.

**02 / NO MORE SECRETS**
Mind readers would uncover millions of secrets. And anyone invisible would be almost impossible to catch.

**03 / SUPER ARMIES**
Within days, governments would recruit the most powerful. Why build fighter jets when someone can fly faster than sound?

**04 / A NEW ORDER**
Your place in society wouldn't depend on wealth or education. Only on the power you randomly received.

**05 / THE ONE**
But among eight billion people, someone could get a power stronger than everyone's. Control time. Manipulate reality. Steal other people's powers.

**06 / THE LUCKY ONE**
Humanity's greatest threat wouldn't be a disaster or an alien invasion. It would be an ordinary person who got incredibly lucky. If you could choose one superpower, what would it be?
