**Cold open:** What would happen if all 8 billion humans suddenly became immortal?

**01 / THE GIFT:** At first, it would seem like humanity's greatest achievement. No more death. No more funerals. No more fear of losing someone.

**02 / ONE PROBLEM:** But there's one massive problem. People would keep being born, while nobody would ever die.

**03 / OVERCROWDED:** Within centuries, Earth's population could grow to unimaginable numbers. Food would become scarce. Cities would become overcrowded. And entire countries could collapse.

**04 / TRAPPED:** But overpopulation wouldn't even be the worst part. Imagine being trapped beneath a collapsed building. You can't move. You can't escape. And you can't die. You could remain there for thousands of years.

**05 / THE DYING SUN:** And even if humanity somehow survived for billions of years, the Sun would eventually make Earth uninhabitable. But you'd still be alive.

**06 / THE LAST LIGHT:** And as the universe slowly grows colder and darker, you'd face something far worse than death. An eternity with no escape.

**07 / THE CURSE:** Because immortality might not be humanity's greatest gift. It might be its greatest curse.

- Hook: a sunny morning, Slavoski on a ladder hanging grandma's "HAPPY 120TH" banner; the ladder tips, he falls flat… and sits straight back up. Title on the second shot. Then a heart monitor's flatline beeps back to life.
