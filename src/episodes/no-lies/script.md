**Title:** WHAT IF HUMANS / COULDN'T LIE?

**Cold open.** Every affair, every fake friendship, and every government cover-up is about to be exposed.
Because humanity has just lost the ability to lie.

**01 / A PERFECT WORLD.** At first, this might sound like the perfect world. No more scams. No more false
promises. No more manipulation through lies.

**02 / FALLING APART.** But within hours, relationships would begin falling apart. Your friends could no
longer pretend to like you. Employees could no longer pretend to enjoy their jobs. And politicians would
have to carefully avoid questions they couldn't answer truthfully.

**03 / NOWHERE TO HIDE.** Within days, criminal investigations could become much easier. Businesses would
struggle to advertise misleading products. Even something as simple as complimenting someone's appearance
could become impossible.

**04 / THE SILENCE.** Eventually, humans would learn to stay silent rather than reveal uncomfortable truths.
Because being unable to lie doesn't mean you're forced to speak.

**05 / KIND LIES.** And humanity would discover something disturbing. Not every lie is designed to hurt
someone. Sometimes, we lie to protect the people we love.

**06 / THE TRUTH.** Maybe a world without lies wouldn't be a perfect world. Maybe it would be a world that
couldn't handle the truth. Would you want to live in it?

- Time of day: sunset (secrets caught in spotlights) → neon night → night interiors → grey morning and rain →
  silent snowy blue hour → a warm kitchen on a snowy night → dawn on a rooftop.
- Generic world, non-graphic: no real people, parties, flags or brands; the "affair" is two people caught by a
  spotlight at a motel door.
