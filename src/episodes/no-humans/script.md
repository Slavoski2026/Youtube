## Fact check
- **Cars:** fine. Tens of millions of vehicles are on the road at any moment worldwide.
- **Planes:** fine. About 10,000+ aircraft are airborne at any time. Autopilot holds course until the fuel runs out; autoland needs a pilot to set it up, so most would eventually crash.
- **24 h, lights out:** fine. Fossil-fuel plants trip offline within hours without operators, and grids lose balance; nuclear plants shut down automatically. Some hydro, solar and wind keep running for a while, so "parts of the world" is the right wording.
- **1 week, tunnels:** ✎ **understated.** New York's subway pumps remove millions of litres a day; estimates say tunnels would flood within about 36 hours. Suggested fix: "would already be flooded".
- **1 month / 1 year / 100 years:** fine, matches common projections. Weeds in cracks within weeks; freeze–thaw, rust and rot in years; forests and roof and building collapses within a century.
- **Thousands of years:** fine. Plastics can last hundreds to thousands of years; stone monuments (pyramids, Mount Rushmore) far longer.
