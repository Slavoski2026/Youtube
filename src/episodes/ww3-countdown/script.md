**Title card:** WHAT IF WW3 / STARTED IN 7 DAYS?
In seven days, World War Three begins. And your life falls apart before a single missile is launched.

**01 / THE ALERT** (day one): Day one. Your phone screams: "Global conflict imminent." Supermarkets are emptied. Petrol queues stretch for miles.

**02 / THE CRASH** (day two): Day two. Stock markets crash. Flights are cancelled. Borders close.

**03 / OFFLINE** (day three): Day three. The internet goes down. ATMs stop working. You try calling your family. Nothing.

**04 / SEEK SHELTER** (day four): Day four. Military jets roar overhead. Another alert: "Seek shelter immediately."

**05 / SILENCE** (day five): Day five. The streets are empty. Shops are boarded up. And the silence is more terrifying than the noise.

**06 / THE LAST MEETING** (day six): Day six. World leaders meet one last time. Then the broadcast cuts to black.

**07 / MIDNIGHT** (day seven): Day seven. 11:59 PM. Your phone vibrates: "Incoming missile warning." Three. Two. One. The sky turns white. And then… nothing. Because the scariest part isn't knowing when it starts. It's not knowing if you'll be there when it ends.
