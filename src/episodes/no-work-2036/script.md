**Title card:** WHAT IF WORK / ENDED IN 2036?
What if 2036 is the year humanity never has to work again? No more nine-to-five. No more six a.m. alarms.
Elon Musk predicts AI and robots could make work optional within ten to twenty years.

**01 / FULLY AUTOMATED:** Welcome to 2036. Your phone buzzes: "Your job has been fully automated." You panic. Then you look outside.

**02 / THE MACHINES:** Robots are building skyscrapers. Trucks drive themselves. Whole factories run without a single human.

**03 / FREEDOM:** Imagine AI so productive that governments pay everyone a universal high income. Travel the world. Start a business. Finally see your family.

**04 / THE CATCH:** But what if the companies that own the robots keep the money? What if millions lose their jobs before help arrives?

**05 / TWO FUTURES:** In ten years: cheap homes and incredible medicine. Or a handful of companies controlling almost everything. Both start with the same invention.

**06 / WHO BENEFITS?:** The real question isn't whether AI takes our jobs. It's who benefits when it does. What do you think: humanity's greatest achievement… or its biggest mistake?

Visual rules: Elon Musk is named in the narration only, never shown (no likeness, no logos of his
companies). Companies on screen are generic (no real brands). Robots are our own low-poly design.
