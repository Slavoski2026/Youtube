**Title card:** WHAT IF EVERYONE / HAD A DEATH / COUNTDOWN?

**Cold open:** There's a countdown floating above everyone's head. Your friend has 64 years left. Your neighbour has 12. But yours says 47 seconds. What would happen if every human could suddenly see exactly when they would die?

**01 / THE NUMBERS APPEAR:** Within hours, society would descend into chaos.

**02 / FEARLESS:** People with decades remaining might become fearless, believing nothing could kill them early.

**03 / LAST DAYS:** Meanwhile, those with only days left would abandon their jobs and spend their remaining time with loved ones.

**04 / A WORLD OF TIMERS:** Insurance companies would become almost obsolete. Employers might refuse to hire people with short countdowns. Even relationships could change, with people choosing partners based on how long they have left.

**05 / CHEATING FATE:** But then scientists would investigate something terrifying. Can the countdown actually be changed? If you avoid a dangerous situation, does your timer increase? Or was your decision to avoid it already part of your fate?

**06 / THE MIRROR:** Now imagine looking in the mirror. Your countdown suddenly drops from 60 years to 10 seconds. And you have absolutely no idea why. Would you rather know exactly when you'll die… or live without ever knowing?

## Visual plan

- Hook (sunny morning street): a timer over a stranger in close-up → a street of people under timers (title) →
  the friend waving at us (64 YEARS, green) → the neighbour over the picket fence (12 YEARS, amber) →
  Slavoski looks up: **00:00:47** in red, ticking → a city of tiny timer dots → a crowd looking up at theirs.
- Timers are green for 20+ years, amber for 1–20 years, red under a year, and tick live (years, days, hh:mm:ss).
- Slavoski stays out of chapters 1–5 (his 47-second timer would contradict them) and returns for "Now imagine…":
  in the mirror his timer reads 60 years, falls to 10 seconds, counts down… and on "or live without ever
  knowing?" it shatters and the warm light comes back. Non-graphic: nobody dies on screen.
- Arc: sunny morning → overcast chaos → storm rooftop → golden office and sunset picnic → rainy day (insurance,
  interview, café) → night lab, night street, the hourglass → a dark bathroom.
- "SINCE THE TIMERS" readout: 3 HOURS → 2 DAYS → 1 WEEK → 6 MONTHS → 2 YEARS (hidden in the cold open and the mirror).
