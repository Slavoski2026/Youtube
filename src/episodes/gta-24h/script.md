## Fact check
- **Reactions slowing / focus fading / getting worse:** real. After about 17–19 h awake, performance matches a 0.05% blood alcohol level; at 24 h it matches about 0.10% (Dawson & Reid 1997; Williamson & Feyer 2000).
- **Blinking less:** real. Blink rate drops from about 15–20 a minute to roughly 5–7 while staring at a screen ("digital eye strain"), which dries and tires the eyes.
- **Neck and back:** real. Hours in a fixed posture strain neck and back muscles.
- **300 cars, lost a chase to a bicycle:** comedy, so tagged DRAMATISED.
