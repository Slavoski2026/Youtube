**Title card:** WHAT IF EVERY / PIGEON / GOT AN IQ OF 200?
At exactly 9 AM, every pigeon on Earth suddenly gets an IQ of 200. At first, nobody notices.

**01 / THE STARE:** Three hours later, pigeons stop eating bread. They start watching humans. By midday, thousands gather outside government buildings. Not moving. Just staring.

**02 / GRIDLOCK:** By 6 PM, they've figured out traffic lights. Coordinated flocks block the intersections. Cities freeze.

**03 / THE NETWORK:** On day two, pigeons start stealing smartphones. Not randomly. They're collecting them, to build their own network.

**04 / WINGED ARMIES:** By day three, millions of pigeons form armies. They occupy airports and military bases. Flights stop. Governments try to negotiate. One problem: nobody speaks pigeon.

**05 / THE MESSAGE:** On day five, thousands of pigeons spell out a message across London's Trafalgar Square: "NO MORE BREAD." World leaders are confused. Until a second message appears: "WE WANT THE BAKERIES."

**06 / THE BREAD WAR:** By day seven, pigeons control the world's bread supply. Humanity has lost a war against birds. And the worst part? They haven't even learned to use weapons yet.
