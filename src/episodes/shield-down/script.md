**Title card:** WHAT IF EARTH / LOST ITS SHIELD? / FOR 24 HOURS
Earth has an invisible shield. And if it disappeared tomorrow, we'd have just 24 hours without it.

**01 / ABSOLUTELY NOTHING:** The first thing you'd notice? Absolutely nothing.

**02 / THE SHIELD IS DOWN:** But thousands of kilometres above you, everything would be changing. Charged particles from the Sun would interact with Earth's atmosphere and satellites differently.

**03 / RADIATION:** Astronauts could face dangerous radiation exposure.

**04 / SOLAR STORM:** And if a powerful solar storm struck at the same time, satellites and communications systems could suffer serious disruption.

**05 / THE LONG GAME:** But here's where it gets terrifying. Earth's magnetic field doesn't just protect technology. Over geological timescales, it also influences how our planet interacts with the solar wind.

**06 / STILL BREATHING:** Fortunately, losing it for one day wouldn't make our atmosphere disappear. And you wouldn't suddenly burn alive.

**07 / EXPOSED:** But imagine looking up at the sky, knowing that one of Earth's greatest natural defences had vanished. For 24 hours, our planet would be far more exposed to the dangers of space. And the scariest part? You might never even realise it happened.

Visual rules: the hook is **personal, not Earth from space**: Slavoski's compass needle lets go in the
first half-second (and swings home on the last line, bookending the episode). Space diagrams are
vertical (Sun up, Earth below, the solar wind falling down the frame). A "SHIELD DOWN FOR" clock runs
00:00 → 24:00 and the day follows it: sunny morning street → late morning → overcast afternoon (the
storm's phone outage) → sunset → night in the back garden and on a hill under a faint aurora → dawn,
when the shield comes back ("EARTH'S SHIELD: BACK ON"). Mars appears once as the real example of a
planet without a global shield (labelled). Tags: REAL SCIENCE on chapters 3, 5 and 6.
